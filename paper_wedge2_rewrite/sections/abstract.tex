\begin{abstract}
We study the action of \(\GL(V)\) on \(\Gr(2,\wedge^2 V)\) for \(\dim V\le 7\).
Using weak-congruence labels only as names for the chosen representatives, we
record the geometric orbit representatives together with the exact stabilizer
sequence
\[
  1\to K_L\to \Stab_{\GL(V)}(L)\xrightarrow{\rho_L} Q_L\to 1
\]
for every row over an algebraically closed field. The row-by-row geometric
kernel and quotient calculations are verified in a companion Lean~4
formalization, while the finite-field descent and orbit-count identities are
checked with companion Magma scripts. The finite-field tables contain exactly
\(4,6,14,20\) orbits for \(n=4,5,6,7\). Summing the corresponding orbit sizes
gives the whole Grassmannian \(\Gr(2,\wedge^2 \Fq^n)\), so the finite-field
tables are complete. Consequently the numbers of geometric orbits are
\(3,5,11,16\).
\end{abstract}
